01 · About

Fiction,
curiosity
& the human mind.

Aabir Goon is a Bengali and English fiction writer based in Alipurduar, West Bengal, India.

His writing moves across psychological fiction, horror, mystery, fantasy and thriller, often focusing on unusual situations, uncertainty and the stranger sides of human experience.

His books have been independently published through Amazon KDP, including Zero Balance, There Should Be Peace, Adulting is a Scam : and how to win it anyway, Oros, Scriptophobia and A silent junction.

Writing is less about staying inside one genre and more about following an idea wherever it leads.
