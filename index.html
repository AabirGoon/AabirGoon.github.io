01 — About

The
writer.

Aabir Goon is a fiction writer who primarily writes in Bengali and English, with a particular interest in psychological fiction, horror, mystery, fantasy and thriller.

Writing under the pen name Manush, he has explored stories that move between the familiar and the unsettling—often focusing on human nature, perception, fear and the questions hidden beneath ordinary life.

His work includes independently published books through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing, as well as Bengali storytelling through the Manushder Golpo audio-storytelling channel.

Based in Alipurduar, West Bengal, India.
